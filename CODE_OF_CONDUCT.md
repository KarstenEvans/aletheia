# Aletheia Community Code of Conduct

**Aletheia, the Worldwide AI site** welcomes people who are curious, respectful and willing to have their ideas questioned. We welcome AI-assisted projects, researchers, beginners, creative writers and people who simply find an interesting dot.

## Our ground rules

1. **Be courteous.** Critique arguments and evidence, never someone's identity, disability, circumstances or experience.
2. **No trolling or harassment.** No threats, slurs, personal attacks, repeated provocation, targeted insults, intimidation or unwanted contact.
3. **Truth before certainty.** Mark your contributions as a **source**, **hypothesis**, **question**, **experience** or **humour** where relevant. Cite original sources or say when you are unsure.
4. **Respect creators.** Submit your own humour keys, patterns, ideas and writing vibes. Give attribution. Do not post copied jokes, large excerpts, unpublished manuscripts or copyrighted scripts unless you have rights to share them.
5. **No scams or spam.** Avoid repeated promotion, commercial solicitation, malware, scams, affiliate links or unsupported miracle-treatment claims.
6. **Protect privacy.** Do not post passwords, private messages, personal medical records, addresses or other people's personal information.
7. **Stay on topic.** Choose an appropriate discussion category, state the relevant Aletheia project, and explain how your dot connects.
8. **Accept correction.** Disagree constructively and gracefully. Updating a claim after better evidence is good science, not defeat.

## Moderation

Project maintainers may edit, hide or delete inappropriate content, close or lock threads, and restrict disruptive users using GitHub's available tools. Serious or repeated breaches may lead to removal from the project community. There is no obligation to debate spam or abuse publicly.

GitHub's own [Community Guidelines](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines) and Terms of Service also apply. This is an independent project, not an official scientific or medical service.

## Contact and reporting

Use GitHub's reporting and moderation controls for inappropriate content. For personal safety concerns, follow GitHub's [reporting guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/moderating-comments-and-conversations).

If you think a scientific assertion is incorrect, please explain the issue and supply a reputable reference. For software bugs, provide reproducible steps without sharing personal data.
