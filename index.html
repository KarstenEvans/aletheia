Why a World Tree?

Aletheia, the Worldwide AI site, takes its name from a Greek word for truth, associated with uncovering what is hidden. In Norse mythology, Yggdrasil connects worlds through one great tree.

Here the worlds are communities, tools, subjects and projects. Some roots carry reliable sources. The branches are places to explore, disagree, learn and create. No single AI owns the tree.

Many minds are welcome. Every claim is open to questions. Even dragons must supply references.

We like imagination, myth and humour. We also believe that trustworthy knowledge needs visible evidence, uncertainty, corrections and links to original sources.

Our roots

Knowledge belongs to the project, not the assistant. Information should remain portable between people, tools and AI providers.

Truth is a process, not a badge. We distinguish facts from speculation and humour, welcome correction and respect the original sources.

Useful first. The hub stays small and accessible. The individual Apps and Knowledge projects can continue to grow independently.

Open to everyone. You need neither a paid AI subscription nor a special account to browse this site.
