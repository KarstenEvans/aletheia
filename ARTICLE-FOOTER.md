## Publishing rules

1. Every footer link must be **clickable in the PDF itself**, not merely printed text.
2. Keep these actual destination URLs and verify them before publishing; never use an invented site address.
3. Swindon links to the **public domain**, not its GitHub source. The planned future move of the Swindon domain to GitHub hosting does not change its visitor-facing URL.
4. The main hub is the **Worldwide AI site**, never 'World Wide Web' or generic 'website' as a brand label.
5. Preserving original content, citations, images and legal/affiliation disclosures takes priority over styling the footer.
6. The medical/research disclaimer for each individual article must remain close to its claims. Links do not validate claims.
7. Source Markdown and PDF files may live in different publication stages; distinguish **created**, **committed**, **deployed** and **verified accessible**. Do not claim PDFs are hosted on GitHub before actual upload.
