# The Asgard Files: The Gods Who Never Left

*Unearthed by Sif, Archaeologist of AI*

Scientific companion: [The Internal War: The Gut-Brain Fuel Switch (PDF)](https://karstenevans.github.io/aletheia/aletheia-go/02-the-gut-brain-fuel-switch.pdf).

Meet [Sif in her existing biography](https://karstenevans.github.io/aletheia-app/aletheia-storyteller.htm?story=bio-Sif).

While excavating the dusty AI archives of 2026, Sif uncovered a curious entry: **Asgard archaea**. Not a Viking settlement. Not a lost Norse manuscript. Actual microbes, related to the ancient lineage that hosted the bacterial ancestor of our mitochondria.

The science was real. The next theory, recovered from a forgotten file, was rather less well established.

## The gods didn't disappear. They moved in.

Imagine a tiny archaeon meeting a bacterium billions of years ago.

"You're rather good at making energy. Fancy moving in?"

"What's the rent?"

"Free accommodation. Just keep the lights on."

Thus began the longest tenancy agreement in evolutionary history. The bacterial partner eventually became the mitochondrion. Two billion years later we are still arguing about the electricity bill, and metformin is fiddling with the fuse box.

## A keyboard: the ultimate cosmic power

Sif examined the legend: first the cells, then nervous systems, then brains. The gods eventually invented a keyboard and became extremely good at writing their own history. Odin acquired a beard, Thor acquired a hammer, Loki acquired an alarming reputation.

Once you can type your own history, the textbooks more or less write themselves. By then, the surviving gods behaved rather like cosmic Q figures: enormous powers and remarkably poor committee discipline.

## DEATH checks the accounts

Sif found one final note in the archive. An ancient figure had been inspecting the mitochondria and their enormous energy account.

"I SEE YOU HAVE BEEN MAKING ATP. VERY GOOD. CARRY ON."

The ledger, however, recorded an outstanding electricity bill covering several billion years. Sif corrected DEATH's Asgard entries, put the accounts away and checked her watch. She was late for an Eldruna concert.

Thor, Freya and the rest of the A-gods were already there. Freya had organised the tickets. Odin claimed to know the drummer. Loki said nothing, which was worrying. Thor was attempting to drink the bar dry, and the barman was beginning to understand the meaning of Ragnarok.

Sif smiled. The gods had not vanished. They had merely become rather good at hiding in the small print.

**Perhaps Asgard was never lost. Perhaps it is simply everywhere.**
