What happens on a GoWalkabout?

Wander. Explore up to three distinct connections, including one that might surprise you. Challenge. Ask what could be wrong, missing or already solved. Wake. Keep only ideas with a reasonable use, honest uncertainty and a small next test.

Different AIs can exchange handoffs. They should not become a polite echo chamber. An independent counterexample beats three enthusiastic compliments. GoWalkabout is bounded research and creativity, not proof of a new discovery.
