# The AI Mirror House 🪞

Look in the same mirror every morning and you see the same face.

Give AI the same sort of question every day and, sooner or later, you may see the same polished phrases, the same sensible suggestions, and the same astonishing number of things apparently “unlocking the future”.

Switching AI applications can help. New mirror, new lighting, different filters, perhaps even an impressive hat.

But another mirror can still show you the same reflection.

That is why we're exploring three complementary ideas:

🌳 **Aletheia Protocol** helps us preserve sources, uncertainty, different interpretations and human control when a piece of work moves between AI systems.

🎭 **Thalia Protocol** brings humour cognition, surprise and a little colour. Not jokes for the sake of jokes, and never a substitute for facts.

🧭 **Aletheia GoWalkabout** asks AI to explore a neglected path, challenge an assumption, borrow a useful idea from another field, and come back with possibilities that survive a reality check.

My favourite part? Give one AI's answer to another, but don't ask it to agree. Ask it to find what the first one missed. Let the mirrors talk, or even gossip a little, provided someone keeps the receipts.

AI originality isn't a competition to open the most browser tabs. Nor is similarity always plagiarism: often it's familiar patterns, repeated prompts and converging ideas. The way out is more curiosity, different perspectives, better evidence and a stronger human voice.

**If your AI work begins to look like a Hall of Mirrors, step outside and Go Walkabout.**
